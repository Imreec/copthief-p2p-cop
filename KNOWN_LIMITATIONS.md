# Known limitations — honest disclosure

Catalogued deliberately: a documented limitation is cheap, a hidden one a grader finds is fatal
to credibility on everything else. Severity: **high** = could change a result's meaning ·
**med** = narrows what the evidence proves · **low** = cosmetic/operational. Each entry says
why it was accepted rather than fixed.

| ID | Severity | Limitation | Why accepted |
|----|----------|------------|--------------|
| L-01 | med | **At-least-once inbound tolerance was live-drilled in one direction only.** The them→us duplicate was absorbed over a real tunnel (M7-8, evidence in `docs/evidence/m6-chaos.md`); the us→them-dedup and live-reorder halves were never drilled on a real edge — they are covered by the keyless chaos battery only. | Ten counted series settled cleanly with zero sequencing incidents; the practical risk retired itself before the drill was scheduled. |
| L-02 | med | **A scent-physics mismatch we raise against a reference-derived opponent may be self-inflicted.** The reference implementation has no step-continuity check and applies a redelivered turn twice; a duplicate *we* sent could corrupt their field and read as their physics error. | Evidence-grade only by design: the physics check never touches verdicts (SQ3); the audit stays the only judge. Finding kept on record in `docs/evidence/m6-chaos.md`. |
| L-03 | med | **One opponent arm sits below the mimic-fidelity bar.** The bestteam-thief arena arm reproduces their own per-step scores at 81% (their code reproduces their tapes at 98%); the 90% floor is CI-pinned (`tests/role/test_bestteam_thief.py`) and every number against that arm is labeled *directional* (ADR-0017). | The arm still bounded the decision correctly (live conversion matched the directional prediction's class); rebuilding to ≥90% was not worth the marginal information. |
| L-04 | low | **A counted determinism leak: two sub-games played byte-identically** (g04 == g06 vs best2934). The seed-consuming tie-breaks (M9-5) are inert when expectimax values separate — identical starts can then replay identically. | Recorded in ADR-0016; no rule is violated and no information leaks that the opponent does not already have (they saw both games). A per-sub-game seed perturbation would fix it. |
| L-05 | low | **The overlay exporter cannot render every opponent's logs.** `copthief overlay` reads revealed cells under our `position` spelling and the two-int `state` form; opponents who reveal a JSON-*string* `state` (ali-ahm1) or a digest with no cell (anrbj666) yield no truth path, so their series' overlays cannot be exported. The GIF renderer (`scripts/render_replay_gif.py`) carries a display-only tolerant reader; the verdict-relevant readers deliberately keep the stricter shape test (M7-58). | Audit verification — the part that matters — is unaffected; figures regenerate from the series whose logs are readable (6 of 10 have full wire logs committed). |
| L-06 | low | **One MCP session per outbound call.** Our client opens a fresh session for every push — the behaviour that stressed one opponent's server into silence (M7-57). The fix shipped was a deadline over the whole exchange *including teardown*; session reuse was never implemented. | With the teardown bounded, the failure mode is contained on our side; session reuse is an optimization, and post-league there is no wire left to optimize. |
| L-07 | low | **An honestly-crashed game replays TAMPERED.** A log truncated by a real crash is indistinguishable, at replay, from a dishonestly-truncated one — the verifier refuses both. | Correct by design (rule 19's "no almost-match"); disclosed so nobody reads a crashed rehearsal log's verdict as evidence of dishonesty. Watchdog snapshots preserve the crash context separately. |
| L-08 | low | **The wire-shape mutual ADR was never co-signed.** ADR-0010's DoD (both teams' signatures on the shape registry) was overtaken by events: the counted series against that partner played and cross-agreed byte-level anyway, and one-counted-per-opponent means no counted game against them remains. | Recorded as closed-as-overtaken (M7-0); the kit registry documents both shapes regardless. |
| L-09 | med | **The strategy instruments do not model the verbal channel.** Arena arms and sweeps model movement, scent, claims and walls; the ≤15-word hint channel (and any opponent's hint-parsing behaviour) is unmodelled — a sweep can not price a hint-based lever. | The fielded agents' hint handling is conservative (closed vocabulary in, template bank out), so the unmodelled channel is narrow on both sides; pricing it was never worth an arm's build cost. |
| L-10 | low | **A counted opponent's report was filed three days late — theirs, not ours.** Our best2934 series (counted #6, the 47–47 tie) was reported by us on time on 2026-08-15; the opponent's symmetric filing silently failed and was delivered on 2026-08-18 after we surfaced the gap from their public tracker. Both corrected filings agree on every field and predate the deadline, so no rule-35 contradiction stands; the exchange is on their tracker's record. | Outside our control; documented so the asymmetry in filing timestamps the lecturer holds has its explanation on our side of the record too. |

Two candidate limitations that are **not** limitations, stated to prevent misreading: the belief
filter's weaker numbers under `multiplicative_book_v1` (argmax 0.609 vs 0.977) reflect that
model's genuinely lower information content, not a filter defect — the innovation fix (M7-14) is
the recovery, and the reference-form path is byte-identical to its M3 baseline. And the thief-side
strategy evidence lives in the sibling repo by design (role work stays in its role repo) — see
[copthief-p2p-thief](https://github.com/Imreec/copthief-p2p-thief).
