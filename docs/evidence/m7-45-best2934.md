# M7-45 — measuring the best2934 matchup before the counted series

**Question.** We meet best2934 in a counted six-sub-game series. On the App F table
(capture 20/5, survival 5/10, three sub-games in each role) the series is decided by one
quantity only: **the differential in cop capture rates**. If both cops convert everything
it is 75–75; if neither does it is 45–45. So: how often does our cop take their thief,
and how often does their cop take ours?

**Method.** Their brains are published (github.com/Krayz1a/best2934-cop, MIT) and were
reimplemented as arena arms — `best2934-police`, `best2934-thief` — carrying the values
they FIELD, not their class defaults. Head-to-head over **64 seeded start scenarios** per
pairing, referee-resolved but **belief-driven**: each side sees only a `BeliefFilter` fed
by the opponent's honest `subtractive_chebyshev_v1` trail. Reproduce with a two-by-two
roster over `scripts/arena_run.py`.

## Fidelity check — the port reproduces their own published number

Their `results/sweep.json` reports a shipped-baseline capture rate of **1.000** for their
cop against their thief. Ours:

| police | thief | captures | survivals |
|---|---|---|---|
| `best2934-police` | `best2934-thief` | **64** | 0 |

64/64 = 1.000, independently. That is the strongest evidence available that the arms model
their policy rather than our guess at it.

## The head-to-head

| police | thief | captures / 64 | capture rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `police-brain` (ours) | `best2934-thief` | **58** | **90.6%** |
| `best2934-police` | `thief-brain` (ours) | **47** | **73.4%** |
| `police-brain` | `thief-brain` (both ours) | 59 | 92.2% |
| `best2934-police` | `best2934-thief` (both theirs) | 64 | 100% |

**Our cop converts 90.6% against their evader; their cop converts 73.4% against ours.**

## Why their evader is the softer half — three structural findings

Pinned as tests in `tests/unit/strategy/test_best2934_thief.py`, all consequences of the
board we negotiated rather than of anything they did wrong:

1. **The area term is inert.** Their docstring calls reachable area "the dominant term".
   `reachable_area(cell, limit=60)` counts CELLS and the board has 49, so the cap never
   bites; and every destination the thief can reach stays connected to every other
   *through the thief's own cell*, which the flood fill never removes. One identical
   number for every candidate, cancelling out of the comparison.
2. **The adjacency penalty is inert.** "Within one step of the belief peak" is "distance
   to the peak ≤ 1", which a distance-maximiser already ranks last. It re-punishes cells
   the main term has rejected, and can only bite when *every* legal destination is
   adjacent — geometry in which the thief is already lost.
3. **The endgame switch cannot change a choice.** It rescales distance 1.2 → 2.0 and area
   0.25 → 0.05. Area being inert, it multiplies the sole live term by a positive constant,
   which cannot move an argmax.

So their shipped evader reduces to a **greedy distance-maximiser that will not camp** —
asserted cell by cell against `greedy-manhattan`. Their cop, by contrast, is genuinely
tuned (a parameter sweep plus a cross-opponent robustness check selecting by best worst
case) and has two live enclosure captures against gal-roy1 to show for it. The asymmetry
in our favour is that they invested in the half that our thief has to survive, and shipped
defaults in the half our cop gets to hunt.

## What this does NOT measure — read before quoting the number

- **No verbal channel.** The arena runs `hint_trust = 0`, so their rationed bluffing and
  their adaptive trust estimator are both unexercised, as is our lie-rate profiler. Their
  trust machinery is the part of their design this study says nothing about.
- **Referee-resolved capture.** Live play resolves captures through the rule-46/47
  claim-and-response path; our M7-19 quiet-cop gate is inert under `subtractive_chebyshev_v1`
  in any case.
- **Arena optimism is documented.** Our cop has led this table throughout the period in
  which it captured nothing at all against anrbj666's tuned evader across two live windows.
  The arena is a ranking instrument, not a prediction of a live scoreline.
- **Their tree can move.** Every number here is their published HEAD on 2026-08-07. Their
  thief is one config line from being tuned, and that is the line that would erase our edge.
